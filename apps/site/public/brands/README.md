# Product marks

Unmodified official project marks, used to identify the subjects of the handbooks.
These marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- `opentelemetry.png`: OpenTelemetry, from https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry.io/blob/main/static/img/logos/opentelemetry-icon-color.png
- `kubernetes.svg`: Kubernetes, from https://github.com/cncf/artwork/blob/main/projects/kubernetes/icon/color/kubernetes-icon-color.svg

The diagrams in `HandbookArtwork.astro` are original OpenLabs illustrations, not product logos.
