What a lab is

Every lab answers one concrete question, and it answers it four times — at four depths. The outside view shows services and telemetry flowing. Go one level down and you are inside a component. Another, and you are on the wire, in HTTP/2 frames. Another, and you are reading the OTLP payload field by field, with whatever just changed still highlighted.

Those are not four drawings. They are four views of one running state, so what you read at the bottom is what produced the picture at the top. When a lab makes a claim, there is a docker compose in the repository that lets you check it against a real Collector.

Sourced from the OpenTelemetry specification and official docs. The teaching order follows Learning OpenTelemetry, because the order in which the concepts hold each other up is right there.
